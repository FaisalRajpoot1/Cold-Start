The Problem

Setup guides rot. Nobody notices, because the people who wrote them never follow them again.

Now teams point AI agents at those guides. Agents are very good at getting around a broken instruction without telling you.

Why It Matters Beyond Docs

The overrule rate generalises. Every agent-completed ticket could carry an independent deterministic check and a number:

“your agent claimed 40 done. 6 were not.”

The check is the verdict. The agent's opinion is only evidence.
